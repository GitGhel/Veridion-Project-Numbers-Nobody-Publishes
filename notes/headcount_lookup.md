# Headcount lookup sheet (ladder tier 2)

Fill in a headcount **band** for each licensee you put in the sample. You don't need all 100: tick `S` for the
~20–25 you choose, making sure every trap group below is represented. The groups are a first sort; move rows if you disagree.

## How to fill it in

**Band**: use LinkedIn's bands so every row speaks the same language:
`0` · `1` · `2–10` · `11–50` · `51–200` · `201–500` · `501–1k` · `1k–5k` · `5k–10k` · `10k+`.
If a source gives an exact number (annual report, registry accounts), write the number; I'll band it.

**Scope**: the column that matters most. Write what the number actually counts:
- `E` = this legal entity only (e.g. UK Companies House accounts of *Siemens Mobility Limited*)
- `G` = the whole group (e.g. LinkedIn page "Siemens", or the parent's annual report)
- `?` = can't tell

LinkedIn usually counts the whole group. A group figure attached to a single subsidiary is the
"small-tier licensee, multinational group" trap the client is worried about, so it must be visible.

**Source**: URL or document name + year. The auditors want evidence, so "LinkedIn" alone isn't enough; give the page URL.

### Where headcounts live (best first)
| Source | What you get | Scope | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual report / 10-K ("Human capital", "Employees") | exact number, year-end or average | G (sometimes split by country) | listed companies, big groups |
| **UK Companies House** (find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk) | "average number of employees" in the filed accounts, even for micro firms | **E** | every GB row |
| **Germany: Unternehmensregister / Bundesanzeiger**, or northdata.de as a free summary | employees for medium/large firms; small firms often show none | E | DE rows (e.K. and small KG may file nothing) |
| Luxembourg Business Registers (lbr.lu) | filed accounts | E | LU holdings |
| Forbes "America's Largest Private Companies" | revenue + employees | G | big US private firms |
| LinkedIn company page → "Company size" | band, plus "N associated members" | usually G | everything, especially startups |
| Press / funding announcements, Dealroom, Crunchbase | "team of N" | G | startups |
| Public-body annual reports / board papers | exact staff count | E (the body) | TfL, NHS, Port Authority, etc. |
